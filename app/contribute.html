Phillips Black is committed to the aspiration of a justice system where “the kind of trial a man gets [no longer] depends on the amount of money he has.” Griffin v. Illinois, 351 U.S. 12, 19 (1956) (Black, J.). The crucial safeguard for fair capital proceedings is post-conviction litigation. Your contribution will enable zealous advocacy in future post-conviction cases where the stakes could not be higher, yet the condemned individuals receive no court-provided support for legal representation.

Please consider making a tax-deductible contribution today.

Your contact information is required to send you a record of your contribution. We will never share your information with others. If you prefer to contribute by mail or telephone, please contact us first at contribute@phillipsblack.org.
